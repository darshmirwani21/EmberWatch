## 24-hour scenarios and shared barrier physics

Ordinary Responder lines were stored and drawn as annotations, while the spread loop still used the unimpeded radial footprint. Historical barriers intercepted coarse center-to-center paths, so a short line could fall between every available connector. A zero acreage difference remains possible when a line is behind the fire, out of reach, or bypassed before it changes a sampled footprint.

- **Runtime.** `shared/simulationTime.ts`, `shared/contracts.ts`, `shared/businessContracts.ts`, `shared/businessExposure.ts`, `src/App.tsx`, `src/ScenarioPanels.tsx`, and `src/SimulationDock.tsx` extend Civilian and Business to a default 1,440 minutes. The radial engine computes 289 five-minute frames, with the same wind shift, footprint, selected-point, and asset calculations. Controls still permit shorter studies. Accelerated playback displays a subset of the cached frames at 24× and 60×. Ordinary Responder and historical interventions retain their existing four-hour maximum.
- **Barrier model.** `shared/firelineGrid.ts`, `shared/interventionSimulation.ts`, `shared/scenarioIntervention.ts`, and `shared/engine.ts` use one barrier solver for both Responder workflows. Ordinary branches seed from the paused radial footprint. Historical branches use their existing recorded or reconstructed seed. Both paths use matched seeds, weather, and meshes. The solver subdivides tiles only near drawn lines, tests geographic segment crossings on shared-side neighbor connections, and applies each gate at its modeled completion time. Open ends allow flanking. The solver tracks direct blocking and alternate arrivals and retains earlier burned geometry. A very short line without an exact connector crossing can map to one nearby perpendicular edge.
- **Controls and evidence.** `src/App.tsx`, `src/HistoricalReplay.tsx`, `src/useIntervention.ts`, `src/SimulationDock.tsx`, and `src/style.css` show synchronized cyan baseline and violet intervention layers on the shared map. After the first run, line edits and removal recompute in the worker.
